# SumatraPDF Plus 3.7.69

## 中文

- 改善调整窗口大小时正文滚动条及收藏夹、目录侧栏的闪烁。
- 批量裁剪切换到尚未单独调整的页面时，沿用当前裁剪框。
- 支持调用已安装的 Word／WPS 打开 WPS 文档，调用 PowerPoint／WPS 演示将 PPT、PPTX、DPS 转为静态 PDF 页面显示，保留页面版式；需要可用的 Office 自动化组件。
- Office 转换结果增加多文件会话缓存，并优先使用 WPS 打开金山格式；首次打开仍需要启动 Office 和导出。
- WPS／演示文档的提取目录仅保留在当前会话，点击保存会提示且不写入原文件；关闭后需重新提取。阻止派生 PDF 覆盖 Office 源文件。

[下载 Windows x64 EXE](https://github.com/dengxibo/sumatrapdf-plus/releases/download/3.7.69/SumatraPDF-Plus.exe)。退出程序后替换完整安装目录中的 EXE，保留配置及配套文件。

## English

- Reduce scrollbar and Favorites/Bookmarks sidebar flashing during window resizing.
- Carry the current crop forward to pages without their own crop in batch cropping.
- Use installed Word/WPS for WPS documents and PowerPoint/WPS Presentation to display PPT/PPTX/DPS as static PDF pages. Available Office automation components are required.
- Cache multiple Office conversions during a session and prefer WPS for Kingsoft formats. First opens still require Office startup and export.
- Keep extracted outlines for WPS/presentations in the current session only. Save confirms this without writing the source; outlines must be extracted again after closing. Prevent derived PDF data from overwriting Office sources.

[Download Windows x64 EXE](https://github.com/dengxibo/sumatrapdf-plus/releases/download/3.7.69/SumatraPDF-Plus.exe). Close the application before replacing the EXE; retain settings and supporting files.
