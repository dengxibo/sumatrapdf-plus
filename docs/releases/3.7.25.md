## What's new

**Faster Match-theme on scan books** — DuXiu/Pdg2Pic full-page scans use whole-tile bitmap recolor; paper+ink pages are no longer misclassified as grayscale photos (avoids the heavy picture-book path).

**Faster Acrobat textbooks** — Exploring Our World / similar Acrobat layout PDFs no longer run expensive content probes on vector-heavy pages at open.

**Faster Easy RL / LaTeX figures** — dense-text pages stay on BitmapRecolor; figure pages use wrap and draw non-full-bleed images as originals (no multi-megapixel soft remap on every flip).

**Softer cream illustrated pages** — notebook-style design books (e.g. 小家越住越大) get gentle paper softening instead of steep ink remap that made grids look dirty.

**Picture-book / UI polish** — sharper scanned text; multi photo-rect protect for RAZ B&W portraits; home scrollbar; compact Home tab; stop embedded PDF audio on tab/window close.

## 本版更新

**扫描书匹配主题更快** — 读秀/Pdg2Pic 全页扫描走整页位图重着色；纸面+文字页不再误判为灰度照片（避开重绘本路径）。

**Acrobat 教材打开更快** — Exploring Our World 等 Acrobat 排版 PDF 打开时不再对矢量极重页做内容探测。

**Easy RL / LaTeX 插图翻页更快** — 密文本页仍 BitmapRecolor；大图页 wrap 且非全幅插图原样绘制（翻页不做多兆柔化）。

**奶油色手帐页更干净** — 如《小家越住越大》：轻柔纸面柔化，避免陡峭墨水映射把网格打成脏噪。

**绘本与界面** — 扫描文字更清晰；RAZ 多照片区保护；主页滚动条与紧凑主页标签；关闭标签/窗口时停止内嵌音频。
