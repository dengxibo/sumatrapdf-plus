# SumatraPDF Plus 3.7.26 (2026-08-06)

### What's new

**Match-theme scan fixes** — white-background contract and government scans no longer stay bright white in dark **Match theme**; stacked-strip scans, dual-layer image contracts, and misclassified colorful scans are handled correctly.

**Picture books & MOBI** — RAZ / picture-book pages use consistent dark paper + light text in Match theme; Chinese MOBI/AZW first-line indent is two fullwidth characters in reader style.

### 本版更新

**匹配主题扫描修复** — 合同/公文类白底扫描在暗色「匹配主题」下不再发白；叠条扫描、双层图像合同、误判彩色扫描等版式已修复。

**绘本与 MOBI** — RAZ/绘本页深色纸+浅色字更统一；MOBI/AZW 中文首行缩进两个全角字符。
