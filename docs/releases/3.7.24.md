## What's new

**Chinese dictionary TTS** — speaker button for Chinese lookups; polyphone tabs speak the selected reading (SSML `<phoneme alphabet="sapi">`); hide the duplicate phonetic line when pinyin tabs are shown; optional `tts-pronunciation.json` for read-aloud overrides (see `tts-pronunciation.sample.json`).

**PDF Match-theme dark mode** — clearer routing: colorful picture-book art keeps natural colors with grey paper softening; only true paper scans use AdaptiveDocument recolor; text-heavy literature keeps OKLab text remapping.

**Screen annotation audio** — play embedded MP3 when the media rendition is on an `/A` Rendition action (Media Clip `/D`), e.g. RAZ-AA *The City* speaker icons.

---

## 本版更新

**中文词典发音** — 查词弹窗支持喇叭朗读；多音字切换拼音标签按所选读音朗读（SSML）；有拼音标签时不再另显一行拼音；可选 `tts-pronunciation.json` 纠正全文朗读（示例见 `tts-pronunciation.sample.json`）。

**PDF 匹配主题暗色** — 分流更清晰：绘本彩色插图保留原色、纸面柔化为灰；仅对真扫描页做 Adaptive 重着色；文字类文学书继续 OKLab 重映射。

**Screen 注释音频** — 支持 `/A` Rendition 动作中的 Media Clip `/D` 内嵌 MP3（如 RAZ-AA《The City》扬声器图标）。
