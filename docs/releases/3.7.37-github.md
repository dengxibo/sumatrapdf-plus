### What's new

**Find** — While find is open, a strip beside the scrollbar marks hits. Blue ticks are every hit, and the orange tick is the current hit. Closing find gives that width back to the page. In continuous view, a precision-touchpad delta smaller than one notch accumulates into pixels. A full mouse-wheel notch still scrolls by lines, and turns the page at the edge.

**Dark Match-theme** — Paper inside a colored textbook frame becomes the theme background and the type goes light. Slices, text, and shapes on a full-page photo keep their original colors, including chapter-opener clouds, title shadows, and map labels. The white square around flat color art is knocked out. Cream carried on badges, title plates, and button strips is knocked out with the page. A light chromatic plate that stays light keeps its original type.

**Sidebar** — The title has Bookmarks, Thumbnails, and Favorites. One click switches the column, and the choice is remembered per document. All three can be searched. Bookmarks and thumbnails share one title search, so switching keeps the text and the matching pages. Favorites filter by name. A favorite title that does not fit ends with an ellipsis, the same as a bookmark. A thumbnail click jumps to that page, and the tag is the page number alone. Favorites use the same hover and selection as bookmarks. Plus adds the current page; minus removes it. The close mark closes the column from any of the three views.

**Home** — A right-click opens a menu. Ctrl+click, Shift+click, and Ctrl+A select files. Delete File asks once, in the current language, then moves the file to the Recycle Bin. Selection boxes stay inside their row, include the left and top edges, and sit clear of a cover's shadow. In light themes the box is a muted slate blue.

**Read aloud** — Choosing another bookmark stays on that page. The Follow button on the read-aloud bar returns to the spoken place. Switching tabs pauses PDF embedded audio, the same as read-aloud and EPUB 3 narration. Coming back to that tab can resume it. Starting from the cursor begins at the first letter of an English word, whichever letter was clicked. A Chinese character still starts at the character that was clicked.

**EPUB** — Full-page comic panels, such as in the Bookworms omnibus, each stay on their own page, with no blank page between them.

**Translate** — When Volcengine Translate and the AI API are not set up, translating a selection sends it to the web AI. An authorization failure does the same.

**Also** — Reading Font opens in a normal dialog: a title bar, a search box, a font list, and OK / Cancel. A click only selects a row. Lazy-load inactive tabs is off by default. In single-page view, a right-click in the blank area beside the page shows the same menu as a click on the page.

### 本版更新

**查找** — 查找界面打开时，滚动条左侧留一条位置刻度。蓝色是全部命中，橙色是当前命中。关掉查找后，页面宽度恢复。连续视图里，触控板不足一格的滚动按像素累计。鼠标滚轮一格仍按行滚动，滚到头会翻页。

**暗色匹配主题** — 彩色边框里的课文白纸会换成主题底色，字改成浅色。叠在整页照片上的切片、文字和色块保持原色，章节首页的云朵、标题阴影和地图上的地名也一样。白色方块上的纯色圆章、渐变按钮，周围的白底会抠掉。奶油色底上的徽章、标题板和按钮条不再留下白色卡片。浅黄一类彩色底如果保持原色，上面的字也不反色。

**侧栏** — 标题上是书签、缩略图、收藏。点一个就切换，选择记在这份文档上。三种都能搜索。书签和缩略图共用一次按标题的搜索，切换时文字和筛出来的页都留着。收藏按名称另筛。收藏标题超出宽度时，行尾显示省略号，和书签栏一样。点缩略图跳到那一页，下面只标数字页码。收藏的悬停和选中跟书签同一套。加号收藏当前页，减号去掉。关闭叉在这三种视图下都关掉这一栏。

**主页** — 右键会弹出菜单。Ctrl+单击、Shift+单击、Ctrl+A 可以多选。删除文件先按当前语言确认一次，再进回收站。选中框留在本行内，左边和上边都会画上，封面选中框离开灰色阴影。明亮主题下选中框是低饱和的灰蓝。

**朗读** — 点其他目录条目会留在那一页。要回到朗读位置，用朗读条上的跟随按钮。切换标签时，PDF 内嵌音频会暂停，和朗读、EPUB3 内嵌音频一样。回到该标签后可以接着播。从光标处朗读时，点在英文单词的任意字母上，都从该单词的开头读起。汉字仍从点中的那个字开始。

**EPUB** — 书虫这类整页漫画，每一幅图单独成页，图和下一幅之间不再夹一页空白。

**翻译** — 没配火山翻译或 AI 接口时，划词翻译发给网页 AI。密钥被拒绝时也一样。

**其他** — 阅读字体改成普通对话框：标题栏、搜索框、字体名单、确定和取消。单击只选中。延迟加载未激活的标签页默认关闭。单页显示时，点页面两侧空白的菜单与点在纸面上相同。
