## What's new

**Scan-page OCR** — Toolbar Auto OCR recognizes the current scanned page (off by default). The dropdown has Recognize all pages, Recognize all pages and save, and OCR region. Files with no text layer are recognized automatically, then bookmarks are extracted.

**Extract bookmarks (books & official docs)** — Build a bookmark tree from the printed contents lines. Official documents use heading schema and numbering continuity.

**Printed TOC calibration** — An editor for comparing bookmarks with the book's printed contents and fixing them by hand. Click an entry to jump to its dest page; double-click jumps to that entry on the contents pages. With body text selected, Ctrl+B inserts as the first child if the current item has children, otherwise as the next sibling. Ctrl+Shift+B replaces the current bookmark.

**Bookmark sidebar** — Acrobat-style editing: the drop line indents to the target level; clicking a selected title no longer starts rename (use F2; double-click still jumps); merge or add keeps scroll and expand state.

**Crashes & chrome** — Hovering or clicking a TOC item after extract or edit no longer crashes on a stale outline URI. Inverted scan paper in Dracula stays `#282A36` instead of sharpening to near-black. Dragging the sidebar splitter repaints immediately, with no leftover bits.

## 本版更新

**扫描页 OCR** — 工具栏「自动 OCR」识别当前扫描页（默认关）。下拉可全文识别、全文识别并保存、框选识别。没有文字层时自动识别再提取目录。

**提取书签（书与公文）** — 提取印刷目录行里的目录信息制作书签树。公文按标题格式和编号连续性提取。

**对准印刷目录** — 提供一个界面，方便与书籍的印刷目录对照，手动修改完善标签。单击目录条目跳转到关联页面，双击跳转到所在的目录页面。选定正文后 Ctrl+B：有子项则插成第一条子项，否则平级插在下方。选定正文后 Ctrl+Shift+B：替换当前标签。

**书签栏** — 接近 Acrobat 的编辑：拖放线按目标层级缩进；再点已选中标题不再改名（用 F2；双击仍跳转）；合并或添加后保留滚动和展开。

**崩溃与界面** — 提取或改书签后悬停/单击不再因过期 URI 崩溃。Dracula 下扫描页纸色保持 `#282A36`，不再锐化成近纯黑。拖侧栏分隔条立刻重绘，不再留下残影。

便携版升级前请先完全退出程序。
